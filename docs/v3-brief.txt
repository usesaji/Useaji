SAJI Project Brief | 3.0
Overview
SAJI is a digital community savings platform built around rotating savings groups, commonly known as ajo or esusu.
Previous versions established group savings, Soroban-powered contributions and payouts, and group governance and recovery functionality.
Version 3.0 introduces savings slot trading and takeover, allowing members to transfer their positions under agreed conditions, alongside personal savings functionality for individuals who want to save independently of a group.
Sprint Goal
Extend SAJI with a Soroban-powered savings slot transfer system and individual savings plans using simulated USDC on Stellar Testnet.
Core Features
1. Savings Slot Marketplace
* Allow eligible group members to list savings slots for takeover.
* Display available slots and their contribution obligations.
* Show payout position and remaining schedule.
* Allow interested members to request a takeover.
2. Slot Transfer & Approval
* Verify eligibility of incoming members.
* Apply group approval conditions.
* Transfer slot ownership through Soroban.
* Preserve contribution and payout history.
* Prevent duplicate ownership of the same slot.
3. Personal Savings Plans
* Create individual savings goals.
* Set target amounts.
* Configure flexible or locked savings plans.
* View savings progress and balances.
4. USDC Personal Savings
* Connect non-custodial Stellar wallets.
* Deposit simulated USDC into personal savings contracts.
* Track deposits and withdrawals.
* Verify transactions on Stellar Testnet.
5. Withdrawal Rules
* Allow withdrawals from flexible savings plans.
* Enforce withdrawal restrictions on locked savings plans.
* Release funds when the configured conditions are satisfied.
* Prevent unauthorized withdrawals.

Technical Requirements
* Extend SAJI's existing savings group infrastructure.
* Soroban contract functionality for slot transfer and ownership.
* Simulated USDC personal savings contracts on Stellar Testnet.
* Non-custodial wallet integration.
* Verification of contribution obligations and withdrawal conditions.
* Verifiable Stellar Testnet contract interactions.
Definition of Done
The MVP is complete when:
* Eligible members can list savings slots.
* Available slots display their contribution and payout details.
* Interested members can request a slot takeover.
* Incoming members are checked against eligibility conditions.
* Required group approvals are enforced.
* Soroban transfers slot ownership after approval.
* Existing contribution and payout records remain intact.
* Duplicate slot ownership is rejected.
* Users can create personal savings goals.
* Users can select flexible or locked savings plans.
* Users can deposit simulated USDC.
* Savings balances and progress update correctly.
* Flexible savings withdrawals work.
* Locked savings withdrawal restrictions are enforced.
* Relevant transactions are verifiable on Stellar Testnet.
* A demo video, GitHub repository, and Soroban contract addresses are submitted.
